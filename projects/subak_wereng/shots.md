# Shots: subak_wereng (2008 frames, 30 fps)

World: Bali rice terraces climbing away from the lens (+z), pura air (candi bentar gate + meru) on the left, dirt path
along the front, palms, Gunung Agung far behind. Paddy cells have a state (water / young / green / gold / eaten /
stubble) driven per shot: synchronized = every cell the same, patchwork = mixed.
Cast: Pak Wayan (teal shirt, caping with RED band, bare feet), Pemangku (all white, white udeng), two city officials
(khaki shirt, dark trousers, black cap / side-parted hair, sunglasses), farmers (caping, earth tones), the Hama (cartoon
planthopper: green-brown, big white eyes; hero bug wears a napkin).
